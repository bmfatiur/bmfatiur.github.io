Our Pritilata House team triumphed in the 2019 PUSTDS Freshers’ League Bangla debate tournament.

---

In 2019, I had the honor of being part of the **Champion Team** in the *PUST Debating Society (PUSTDS) Freshers’ League*, an Asian Parliamentary-style Bangla debate tournament. Representing **Pritilata House**, our team brought together voices from across departments and disciplines.

I was the third speaker on a dynamic team alongside:
- **Akhi** from the Statistics Department (Session: 2018–19)
- **Afsana** from the BBA Department (Session: 2018–19)

Together, we navigated rounds of intense argumentation, critical thinking, and teamwork to emerge as champions. This experience sharpened my public speaking and reasoning skills and deepened my appreciation for collaborative leadership.
