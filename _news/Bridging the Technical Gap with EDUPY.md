On **June 14, 2023**, I was honored to receive an **Honorary Crest** as the **General Secretary of Solver Green** during the event _"Bridging the Technical Gap with EDUPY."_ Hosted by Solver Green, this technology-focused student initiative aimed at enhancing campus-wide innovation by bridging the gap between academics and industry.

The program featured:

- **Intra-University Programming Contest**
- **UI Design Competition**
- **Content Writing Challenge**

Participants were awarded **industrial internships**, **crests**, and **certificates** in recognition of their talents and dedication.

This recognition reaffirms my commitment to empowering students through leadership and collaboration, while also contributing meaningfully to technically enriching events that prepare us for real-world challenges.
